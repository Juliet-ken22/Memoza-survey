What LinkUp AI is

The problem. Uganda Cranes matches at Namboole, Blankets & Wine, Nyege Nyege — tens of thousands of people in one place, and the moment it matters the network dies. Your data bundle is finished, the WhatsApp group goes silent, nobody knows who is where. If a child wanders off, if someone collapses in the stand, if the crowd starts to crush at Gate C, the only tools on the ground are shouting, a steward's radio and walking.

The idea. One system that keeps an event safe and keeps the crowd entertained, and that works on any phone. A smartphone attendee uses an app. Someone carrying a torchlight phone — the small keypad ones — uses the same safety net by dialing *384#: no internet, no data bundle, nothing to install. Everything lands with the organisers on a control console, and the same live information goes up on the stadium projector.

How a real day flows

Grace and her two friends arrive at Namboole. She opens the app, enters the event code CRANES2024 and creates a safety circle called "Green Caps" with a PIN and a meeting point — Gate C, food court. Her uncle, who has a basic phone, dials *384#, joins the same event and the same circle with the PIN. Nobody needed internet.

In the second half, a man collapses near the terrace. Someone hits SOS → Medical in the app; over USSD it is option 3 on the menu. The report goes through AI triage that labels it Medical, High priority, and it appears on the organiser's console and as a ticker on the stadium screen. A steward resolves it from the console; the wall stops showing it, and the event health score is recalculated.

Meanwhile the fun side: a live poll asks who the man of the match should be. Vote, and you have a real chance of UGX 5,000 airtime transferred to your number. Grace films 15 seconds of her stand; it goes up on the crowd wall. At half-time the organiser pins the best clip, and a stand camera the steward switched on is live on the same projector.

And when Tom's son gets separated from him, Tom reports the child's name, age and last-seen point from the app or from *384#. It is on the console and the big screen within seconds, and the moment security finds him, one tap marks him reunited and the family gets an SMS.

The four parts

The attendee appCheck in with a code · safety circles with a PIN · one-tap SOS (medical, security, fire, crowd crush) · missing-person board · live polls with airtime · record a clip of your stand · find the nearest meeting point.
*384# on any phoneThe same safety and fan features over USSD: join an event, build a group, raise an SOS, report a missing person, vote in the poll, ask for a meeting point. Works on a 2G network with zero data.
The organiser & staff consoleWhere the day is run from at its own address: incoming reports with filters and one-click resolve, the missing-child log, creating events and join codes, pinning crowd clips, and pushing a staff camera live to the projector.
The stadium wallThe projector view: who is checked in, live safety alerts, the fan poll result, the crowd clip wall, the hype shout-outs and the event health score.
